## What's new

1. **Standing clarified:** Direct Standing, Delegated Participation, and Institutional Representation are distinguished. Standing does not transfer through delegation or an institution.
2. **Collective decision safeguards clarified:** Governance, voice, exit, and Decision Necessity constrain or contextualize decisions but do not authorize closure.
3. **Participation clarified:** Exercise and affirmative authorization content must both be checked. Delegation requires prior, scoped, operative, revocable authority and independent delegate exercise. Cascade authorization is determined per affected standing-holder.
4. **Two independent outputs:** Authorization Status distinguishes AUTHORIZED, WITHHELD, and genuinely UNAVAILABLE; Decision Status is distinct from the Permissibility Verdict. A decision can be REQUIRED while its closure remains IMPERMISSIBLE.

Defensive Exception remains a separate **permissibility basis**, not an authorization route. All applicable safeguards must be satisfied.

## For builders upgrading from v1.2.1

**Do not upgrade by changing the version string alone.** Review the [migration checklist in CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md#migration-from-v121-implementation-changes), update the evaluator and templates, and rerun regression cases involving refusal, incapacity, collective decisions, cascades, and Defensive Exceptions.
