## DEFENSIVE EXCEPTION ANALYSIS

**Exception type:**  
Standard (agent-specific) / Structural / Not claimed

### Standard Defensive Exception — 7 Safeguards
- ⬜ Specificity  
- ⬜ Burden of proof  
- ⬜ Proportionality  
- ⬜ Discrimination  
- ⬜ No self-dealing  
- ⬜ Recursive check  
- ⬜ Sunset and review  

### Structural Defensive Exception — Enhanced Safeguards
- ⬜ Structural threat confirmed  
- ⬜ High-quality evidence  
- ⬜ Narrow tailoring  
- ⬜ Strong sunset & review  
- ⬜ Scope limits  
- ⬜ Independent oversight  
- ⬜ Lower-closure alternatives exhausted  
- ⬜ Aggregate proportionality
